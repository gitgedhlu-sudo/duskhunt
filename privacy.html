Template — review and adapt it to your own situation and local law before launch.

What we collect

A display name you choose and your device's GPS location while you are inside a match. No account or email is required.

How location is used

Your location is sent to the game server only while you are in a match. Opponents see Shadow locations only at ping times, or live if a Shadow leaves the play zone. Teammates see each other live. Positions are shown on the results screen when a match ends.

Retention

Match data is held in server memory only and is deleted shortly after a match ends or after inactivity. It is not written to a database.

Third parties

Map tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap. The map library is loaded from a public CDN.
